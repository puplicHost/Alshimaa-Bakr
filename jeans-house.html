Jeans House "Denim Factory"

Specialized brand in women's jeans production and distribution

Jeans House is a specialized brand in the production and distribution of women's jeans, targeting women aged 18 to 45 who seek a blend of quality, style, and comfort at competitive prices. Since its inception, Jeans House has built a loyal local customer base and is now aiming to expand its market presence and increase its market share in the ready-to-wear fashion industry.
